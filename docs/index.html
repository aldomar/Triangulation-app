Triangulation Locator

An Android app that estimates where a distant object, such as a column of smoke, is by taking compass bearings from two or more places.

Download the app (APK)

Android 8.0 or newer. Free, no ads, no tracking.

This app gives an estimate to guide where to look. It is not a location to send emergency services to. For a real fire, call 911 or your local fire agency.

How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download the app.
  2. If the browser warns that the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway. (Android shows this for every app that doesn't come from the Play Store.)
  3. When the download finishes, tap Open (or find triangulation-locator.apk in your Downloads).
  4. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back.
  5. Tap Install. The app appears in your app list as Triangulation Locator.

Updating

Come back to this page, download again and install over the top. Your saved sessions are kept.

If you installed the app earlier from Android Studio, you'll see "App not installed". Uninstall the old copy first. This deletes its saved sessions, so export any you want to keep before uninstalling.
